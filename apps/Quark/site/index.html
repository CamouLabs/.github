The loop

Practise. Miss. Come back.

Quark is built on three findings that hold up outside the lab, and it is honest about the third one: memory fades, so the path has to be allowed to take a lesson back.

1

Interleaved, not blocked

Sessions ramp from warm-up to stretch, and question kinds are mixed rather than grouped. Mixing costs a little practice accuracy and buys a lot of retention.

2

Answers are re-derived

A small arithmetic parser re-computes every numeric answer instead of trusting it — for the built-in questions and for anything the model proposes.

3

Mastery decays

A forgetting half-life is fitted per lesson. When predicted recall drops below 70%, that lesson is due again — so a crown on the path is earned continuously, not once.

Apple Intelligence, on device

A coach that stays in its lane.

Apple's on-device model writes the hints, the explanations, and the occasional bonus question. It is given the lesson's facts and nothing else — no internet, no other lessons, no room to improvise. Everything it produces is a draft until the guardrails have signed it off.

  • Hints point at the method, never the answer
  • Explanations respond to the answer you actually gave
  • Generated questions are always labelled as generated
  • With the model off, every lesson still has written coaching
How it is fenced

Guardrails

Eleven checks, all readable.

Apple Intelligence has its own safety guardrails, and they run first. These are Quark's own: narrow, deterministic checks on anything the model writes. They are separate from the model, testable without it, and listed in the app in one line each.

Shape

A generated question must have four distinct options and exactly one marked correct.

Safety

Anything violent, sexual, self-harm related, or otherwise unsuitable is dropped, not softened.

No personal data

Names, emails, phone numbers, and links are stripped; the coach never asks for personal details.

On syllabus

Generated material has to use the vocabulary of the lesson it claims to belong to.

Arithmetic re-checked

Numeric answers are re-derived from arithmetic before the question is shown.

One right answer

Duplicate options, "all of the above", and questions that contain their own answer are rejected.

Readable

Long sentences and jargon the lesson never introduced are rejected.

Grounded in the lesson

Every number in coach text must appear in the lesson or the question itself.

Not a repeat

A generated question that repeats one already asked is discarded.

No spoilers

A hint that contains the answer is rewritten once, then replaced with the built-in hint.

Short enough

Coach replies are capped so a hint stays a hint.

"We have guardrails" is a claim, not a fact. So the app keeps a ledger per session and shows it on the summary screen, and the repository ships an adversarial harness that red-teams all eleven with a model that misbehaves on purpose — 36 scenarios, each naming the guardrail that is supposed to catch it. Privacy

Nothing leaves the phone.

Not as a setting, and not as a promise about how the data is handled once it arrives somewhere. Quark makes no outbound requests at all.

On device, always

Lessons, questions, hints, explanations, and grading all happen on your iPhone, through Apple's on-device model.

No network calls

Quark makes none. It works in airplane mode, and there is no account to create.

One local file

Your XP, streak, and per-lesson skill records live in a single JSON file in the app's own container.

Nothing to sell

No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporter, no in-app purchases.
